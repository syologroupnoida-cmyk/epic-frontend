# Content page banner images

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the Epic Wedz public content and legal pages. Final assets are stored in `public/images/content/`; the exact per-image prompts are recorded in `content-banner-prompts.json` beside this document.

The set includes Home, Venues, Vendors, Planner, Destinations, Luxury, Real Weddings, Marketplace, Invitations, Blog, Partner, About, Contact, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions. Each image is original illustrative artwork, not a photograph of a listed vendor, actual customer or bookable venue.

The compositions leave a calmer center for the website's live text. No headings or branding are baked into the images. A dark overlay provides contrast for centered white headings. Shared informational/legal banners are at least 240px high on desktop and 180px on mobile; marketplace search banners have a 260px minimum height.

The legal content card has its natural full height, without an internal scroll area. Contents links scroll the document to the chosen section, and the Privacy Policy menu scrolls normally with the page on the right.
